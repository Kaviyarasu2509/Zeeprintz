# SEO Launch Checklist

Deploy `dist` after `npm run build`. The homepage is prerendered. Canonical, schema and sitemap consistently use https://www.zeeprintz.in/. Avoid conflicting www/non-www redirects.

- Confirm full street address, postcode, opening hours and available services before adding them to visible content and schema. Missing address details may limit LocalBusiness rich-result eligibility.
- Replace design examples with genuine product/shop photographs when available. Do not claim mockups are completed customer orders.
- Check homepage, images, robots.txt and sitemap.xml return HTTP 200 without redirect loops after deployment.
- Validate with Google Rich Results Test and Search Console URL Inspection. Submit https://www.zeeprintz.in/sitemap.xml.
- Keep the verified Google Business Profile name, address, phone, website, categories and services accurate. Ask customers for honest reviews without incentives.
- Build substantial individual service pages with verified materials, finishes, specifications, ordering details and photographs. Add URLs to the sitemap only once those pages exist.
- Monitor impressions, indexing and enquiries. No search ranking position is guaranteed.
